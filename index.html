How the estimate is built

A transparent, editable formula — not a black box

03 — Formula

estimate = area × locality_rate × bhk_factor

The locality rate is an indicative per-sq.ft. benchmark for that neighbourhood. The BHK factor nudges the rate up slightly for larger configurations, which tend to carry a small premium per sq.ft. in the same building. The final figure is shown as a band (±8%) rather than a single number, since actual listings vary by floor, age, amenities and exact street.

This mirrors, at a simple level, how a linear regression model would combine a categorical feature (locality), a continuous feature (area) and an ordinal feature (BHK) into one predicted price.

Locality55%

Locality dominates the estimate — the same flat can differ by more than 2× in price simply by moving across the city. Area scales the value directly, while BHK mostly reflects how that area is divided up.

Note on the data — Locality rates are indicative, illustrative benchmarks for demonstration purposes and do not reflect live listings or verified transaction data. Real prices vary by floor, age, builder, amenities and exact micro-location. For an actual purchase or sale, cross-check current rates on a property portal or with a local broker before relying on any figure shown here.
